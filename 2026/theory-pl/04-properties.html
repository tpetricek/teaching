Language evaluation is deterministic

Theorem (Determinacy)

If \(\langle e, s \rangle \rightarrow \langle e', s'\rangle\) and also \(\langle e, s \rangle \rightarrow \langle e'', s''\rangle\)
then \(e'=e''\) and \(s'=s''\).


Property of evaluation

  • No mention of types - just reduction relation
  • Not necessarily through the same reduction!
  • Does not hold for some languages (e.g., concurrency)

Well-typed programs do not get stuck

Theorem (Safety)

If \(\Gamma \vdash e : \tau\) and \(dom(\Gamma)\subseteq dom(s)\) and \(\langle e, s \rangle \rightarrow^{*} \langle e', s'\rangle\)
then either \(e'\) is a value or there exist \(e'', s''\) such
that \(\langle e', s' \rangle \rightarrow \langle e'', s''\rangle\).


Combination of two properties

  • Progress - a well-typed program is a value or can reduce
  • Preservation - reduction preserves the type of a program

Induction

Proof technique

Three forms of induction

Induction over natural numbers

\(\forall x\in\mathbb{N}.\Phi(x)\) if \(\Phi(0)\) and \(\Phi(x)\Rightarrow\Phi(x+1)\)

Induction over structure of a term

Show that \(\forall e\in\mathbb{E}.\Phi(e)\) by showing \(\Phi(e)\) holds for
leaves and \(\Phi(e_1),\ldots,\Phi(e_n) \Rightarrow \Phi(\texttt{op}(e_1, \ldots, e_n))\)

Induction over a relation

Show that \(\forall x.R(x) \Rightarrow \Phi(x)\) by showing \(\Phi(J)\) holds for axioms (no assumptions) and \(\Phi(x)\) follows from assumptions \(\Phi(x_1), \ldots, \Phi(x_n)\) for each rule \(\dfrac{x_1, \ldots, x_n}{x}\).

Induction

Three properties

  • Determinacy - induction over term structure
  • Progress - induction over \(\vdash\) relation
  • Preservation - induction over \(\rightarrow\) relation
  • Safety - induction over \(\rightarrow^{*}\) relation
